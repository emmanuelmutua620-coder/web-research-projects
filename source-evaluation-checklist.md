# Source Evaluation Checklist

For each source, record:
- [ ] Is the author, publisher, or issuing institution identifiable?
- [ ] Is the source relevant to the research question and geography?
- [ ] Is the publication/update date appropriate for the topic?
- [ ] Does it cite evidence, data, methods, or primary documents?
- [ ] Can key claims be checked against another reliable source?
- [ ] Is the source reporting facts, opinion, advertising, or advocacy?
- [ ] Have limitations or potential bias been noted?
- [ ] Have the exact title, URL, publication date, and access date been saved?
